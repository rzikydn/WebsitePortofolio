## Numbered Findings List

### [01] 3D Memoji Avatar Sticker in Contact Section
- **Violated Rule**: `R-22 — Illustrations` & `R-23 — Clarification & Visual Assets`
- **Rule Tier**: Hard Gate
- **Priority**: **HIGH**
- **Reason**: The Apple-style 3D Memoji avatar winking with a peace sign is a recognizable stock AI portfolio template asset that contrasts sharply with the authentic personal photo on the hero lanyard ID card, undermining professional credibility for a Data Analyst.
- **Suggested Fix**: Replace the sticker with authentic branding, an interactive contact widget, or a clean typography-focused contact card without cartoon 3D assets.

---

### [02] Arbitrary Clustered Floating Stickers in About Section (Dragon, Cassette, CRT TV, Sakura)
- **Violated Rule**: `R-22 — Illustrations` & `R-20 — Visual Identity`
- **Rule Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: **MEDIUM**
- **Reason**: Four unrelated floating objects (oriental dragon, retro cassette tape, vintage CRT TV, sakura flower) are pinned across the About section without conceptual or thematic relevance to Data Analytics / Data Science.
- **Suggested Fix**: Remove the random decorative stickers to give the typography space to breathe, or replace them with subtle domain-relevant data visualization motifs (e.g. clean metric badges, data chart lines).

---

### [03] Background Blueprint / Graph Paper Grid on Hero and About
- **Violated Rule**: `R-07 — Background`
- **Rule Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: **MEDIUM**
- **Reason**: Grid squares and graph paper lines are a default AI trope used to make pages look "technical" without intentional branding.
- **Suggested Fix**: Remove the grid lines or soften them into a subtle, purposeful texture that supports clean readability.

---

### [04] Unanchored Neon Lime Green Curved Doodle in Works Section
- **Violated Rule**: `R-01 — Color & Gradients` & `R-29 — Color Palette`
- **Rule Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: **MEDIUM**
- **Reason**: A bright neon lime green curved SVG stroke cuts across the dark navy background without belonging to the core brand color system.
- **Suggested Fix**: Harmonize the line color with the primary site palette (subtle cyan/slate or brand blue), or remove the stroke if it does not guide user focus.

---

### [05] Highlighter Over-Saturation on About Paragraph
- **Violated Rule**: `R-13 — Glow / Focus Accent` & `R-39 — Interaction Affordances`
- **Rule Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: **MEDIUM**
- **Reason**: Two sentences contain four distinct multi-color highlight effects (orange underline on "analyzing data", blue pill on "creating insights", orange underline on "Data Science", blue pill on "Cakrawala University"), diluting visual hierarchy.
- **Suggested Fix**: Keep at most one focal highlight per paragraph in a unified accent color so the key message stands out clearly.

---

### [06] Repetitive Giant Watermarked Background Typography
- **Violated Rule**: `R-05 — Layout & Page Structure` & `R-06 — Typography`
- **Rule Tier**: Quality Locks
- **Priority**: **LOW**
- **Reason**: Every single dark section ("MY WORKS", "TECHNICAL SKILL", "MY EXPERIENCE", "MY CERTIFICATES", "CONTACT ME") repeats the exact same massive semi-transparent watermark, some of which clip awkwardly ("MY EXPERIENC", "Y CERTIFICATI").
- **Suggested Fix**: Introduce rhythm and variety: make section headers clean and distinct rather than repeating the same oversized background watermark across every section.

---

### [07] Cartoon/Graffiti Tech Stack Logos vs Serious Dark Aesthetic
- **Violated Rule**: `R-20 — Visual Identity`
- **Rule Tier**: Quality Locks
- **Priority**: **LOW**
- **Reason**: The technology logos in the skills marquee feature playful cartoon/sticker outlines and Japanese characters, creating a stylistic mismatch with the serious, elegant corporate portfolio theme.
- **Suggested Fix**: Use official, clean monochrome or refined SVGs for tech logos with consistent padding and scale.

---

### [08] Default Button Arrow on Contact CTA (`Get In Touch →`)
- **Violated Rule**: `R-08 — Button Arrows`
- **Rule Tier**: Purpose-Gate
- **Priority**: **MEDIUM**
- **Reason**: Arrow `→` is appended as default button decoration.
- **Suggested Fix**: Ensure button text is action-focused (e.g. "Send Email" or "Contact Me") or style the arrow with deliberate hover animation.

---

### [09] Oversized Footer Brand Monolith ("RZIKYDN")
- **Violated Rule**: `R-20 — Visual Identity` & `R-06 — Typography`
- **Rule Tier**: Quality Locks
- **Priority**: **LOW**
- **Reason**: Giant viewport-spanning name at the bottom of the footer mimics trendy brutalist templates and dominates the page balance.
- **Suggested Fix**: Proportion the footer branding so it acts as an elegant closing signature rather than dominating the screen.
